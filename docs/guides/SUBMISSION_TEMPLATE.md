# Submitting an Article

Submit your article using the Indigo Magazine Google Form. No coding tools are required.

---

## Quick Steps

1. Open the Indigo Article Submission Form (ask the Writing director for the link).
2. Fill in the details: title, your name, category, and a short summary.
3. Paste your article text into **Article Body**, or upload a Word/Google Doc under **Document**.
4. Upload any photos (including a cover photo) and reference them by file name in the body.
5. Submit the form.

---

## Form Fields

| Field | Required | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Title` | Yes | Headline for the article. |
| `Author` | Yes | Your name as it should appear on the site. |
| `Category` | Yes | Pick one category from the dropdown. |
| `Summary` | Yes | One or two sentences for the article card (under 300 characters). |
| `Date` | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`. Leave blank to use the submission date. |
| `Designers`, `Photographers`, `Graphics` | No | Credits, if applicable. |
| `Cover Image` | No | Upload the main card photo (or type the filename of an uploaded image). |
| `Images` | No | Upload photos used inside the article (keep each under 2 MB). |
| `Article Body` | Yes (unless uploading a Document) | Full article text. |
| `Document` | No | Optional `.docx`, `.txt`, `.md`, or Google Doc upload instead of pasting into **Article Body**. |

---

## Formatting the Article Body

Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

| Element | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Paragraph | Plain text. |
| Section heading | `## Heading text` (use two `#` marks, never one) |
| Photo | `![Description of the photo](photo-1.jpg)` on its own line |
| Photo with caption | `![Description](photo-1.jpg "Caption")` |
| Gallery | Place two or more photo lines back to back with no blank line between them |

**Every photo needs a description** inside the square brackets (`![Description](...)`). Screen readers rely on it and validation requires it.

### Avoid

- Bold, italics, and Markdown links (they render as plain text right now)
- Bulleted or numbered lists
- Tables
- Block quotes starting with `>`

---

## What Happens Next

1. **Logged.** Submitting creates a review folder in Google Drive and adds a row to the writing team's tracking Sheet.
2. **Checked.** A `VALIDATION.md` report is generated automatically listing any errors or warnings.
3. **Reviewed.** An officer reviews the article and shares feedback if changes are needed.
4. **Published.** Once approved, the article goes live on the website automatically.
