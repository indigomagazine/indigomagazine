# How to Submit an Article

You can submit an article by email. You do not need any technical tools.

---

## Quick Version

1. Email the submissions address (ask the Writing director for it).
2. Put the subject as the title of your article.
3. Put a few details at the top of the message, then your article.
4. Attach images if you have them. Refer to them by file name.
5. Send. You will get a reply when it has been received and reviewed.

---

## Step 1: Subject

The subject line is the **title** of your article.

```text
Subject: How To Ruin A First Date!
```

You can start the subject with `Submission:` if you like. It will be removed.

---

## Step 2: Details (top of the message)

Start the message with these lines. Each one goes on its own line.

```text
Category: Culture
Summary: One or two sentences that describe the article. Keep it under 300 characters.
Date: 2026-11-26
Designers: Name and Name
Photographers: Name
Graphics: Name
CoverImage: photo-1.jpg
```

| Line | Required | What to write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Category` | Yes | One category from the magazine's current list. Ask the Writing director which ones are in use. |
| `Summary` | Yes | A short blurb for the article card. |
| `Date` | No | `YYYY-MM-DD`. Leave it out to use the date you send the email. |
| `Designers`, `Photographers`, `Graphics` | No | Credits. Leave them out if they do not apply. |
| `CoverImage` | No | The file name of one of your attached images. It is the picture on the article card. |

Your name is taken from your email account, so you do not need to write it.

---

## Step 3: Your Article

Write the article below the details. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

| You want | Write this |
| --- | --- |
| A paragraph | Plain text. |
| A section heading | `## Heading text` (use two `#` marks, never one) |
| A photo | `![Description of the photo](photo-1.jpg)` on its own line |
| A photo with a caption | `![Description](photo-1.jpg "Caption")` |
| Several photos in a row | Put the photo lines next to each other with no blank line between them. They appear as a gallery. |

**Every photo needs a description** (the text inside the square brackets). It is read by screen readers and it is required.

### Please avoid

- Bold, italics, and links (write plain text; put links in the email's text as plain words)
- Bulleted or numbered lists
- Tables
- Quotes that start with `>`

---

## Step 4: Attachments

- **Photos:** attach them to the email. Use the same file names you wrote in the article. Please use photos under 2 MB each.
- **A document:** if you would rather write in Word or Google Docs, attach the document and leave the body of the email empty. Its text will be used. Headings in the document are not kept yet, so mark section breaks with a `## ` heading line if you can.

---

## What Happens Next

1. **Received.** Your submission is added to the review folder. Anyone on the writing team can read it.
2. **Checked.** The system writes a report called `VALIDATION.md` in your submission folder. It lists anything that needs fixing.
3. **Reviewed.** A reviewer reads the report and sends feedback. A developer approves it.
4. **Published.** Approved articles go live on the website shortly after approval.

Questions? Ask the Writing director.
