Eternal Unfolding

by Zenah Itani

After the time has passed
There is nothing to hold onto,
And no stopping the black from oozing out of every duct,
Purging what has been lost

But now that there's emptiness,
It's all become so clear:
The changing tides,
And the direction that the grass blows

The wind promises nothing
But to carry everything with it
Through the atmosphere, where stars are running

Meanwhile, sweet juice leaks underneath rough feet
And waters the grass trailing behind,
Satisfying every sweet tooth in the dirt

The ambitious one,
Who listens to the deep hum in their chest,
Carries strength in their legs to push themselves
Into a fateful unknown

The spirit that is all becoming,
Sees those dreams in black and white

Fin.
